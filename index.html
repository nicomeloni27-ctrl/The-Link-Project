Connect to ChatGPT

Use your own Link account to connect a ChatGPT app. You can ask ChatGPT to list or create classes and units, save text study sheets, and retrieve your documents.

ChatGPT connection status: check in ChatGPT settings. This website cannot verify that a ChatGPT session is connected.

How to connect: Sign in to The Link, then add the custom MCP app in ChatGPT with server URL https://the-link-project-ten.vercel.app/api/mcp. When prompted, sign in using your existing Link account and approve access. This requires the OAuth setup and ChatGPT custom app access described in the project instructions.

Privacy: Your classes and documents are tied to your own Link account, not the website owner.
